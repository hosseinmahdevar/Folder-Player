# PRD: FolderPlay

*Clean-Room Reconstruction & Functional Specification from Decompiled Source (Smart AudioBook Player v11.9.0)*

## 0. Document Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) provides an exhaustive, clean-room functional and architectural specification for **FolderPlay** (reconstructed from `ak.alizandro.smartaudiobookplayer`, v11.9.0). It is authored for software architects, mobile engineers, QA engineers, and product managers to guide a complete clean-room implementation in **Flutter (Dart)** targeting Android, iOS, and Desktop platforms.

The document specifies the exact domain behaviors, file schemas, user journeys, playback state machines, and tier-gating discovered from the reverse-engineered codebase. Technical file structures, binary storage layouts, and Tasker broadcast actions are cross-referenced in the companion `addendum.md`.

---

## 1. Vision

Audiobooks present unique requirements that standard music players fail to address: narration requires distinct vocal clarity over wide dynamic ranges, sessions span tens or hundreds of files over many weeks, listeners frequently fall asleep mid-track, and pauses cause loss of narrative context.

**FolderPlay** is the definitive standalone, offline-first audiobook playback suite. It treats books as first-class, multi-file atomic entities rather than generic audio playlists. Through intelligent auto-rewind calibrated to pause duration, motion-activated sleep timers with gentle fade-out, neural-network speech enhancement (RNNoise), and non-destructive folder-based metadata storage, it delivers an uncompromising, distraction-free listening experience without cloud lock-in or recurring subscriptions.

## 2. Target User

### 2.1 Jobs To Be Done

- **Context Preservation:** *"When I resume an audiobook after a phone call, a work shift, or waking up in the morning, I want the player to rewind slightly based on how long I was away, so I can immediately regain the narrative thread without scrubbing manually."*
- **Bedtime Listening without Position Loss:** *"When listening in bed, I want the player to automatically fade out after a configured interval, and let me reset the timer simply by picking up or shaking my phone without opening my eyes or looking at a bright screen."*
- **Poor Audio Correction:** *"When an audiobook is recorded at low volume, in high-noise environments, or with jarring speaker volume differences, I want volume boost, pitch tuning, and AI voice de-noising so narration is crisp and fatigue-free."*
- **Multi-File Book Navigation:** *"When a book consists of dozens of ripped MP3 or AAC files, I want the player to treat them as a single continuous piece with unified progress, automatic chapter extraction, and seamless file transitions."*
- **Offline & Storage Efficiency:** *"When storage is constrained, I want to convert space-consuming MP3 books into high-efficiency Opus audio in the background without draining my battery or losing embedded cover art."*

### 2.2 Non-Users (v1)

- **Streaming Cloud Catalog Consumers:** Users seeking a proprietary subscription streaming store (e.g., Audible or Storytel catalog browsing). This application is strictly an offline-first player for user-provided files.
- **Music Listeners:** Users looking for shuffle playlists, beat matching, gapless music crossfading, or ID3 album/artist hierarchies suited for musical tracks.
- **DRM Encrypted File Owners:** Users attempting to play encrypted proprietary formats (e.g., Apple Books FairPlay or Audible `.aax` without prior personal decryption).

### 2.3 Key User Journeys

#### UJ-1. Marco Resumes After an Interruption
- **Persona + context:** Marco, commuting by train, is 14 hours into a 32-hour epic fantasy book spanning 45 separate MP3 files.
- **Entry state:** Audio was paused 4 hours ago when arriving at work. Phone display is off; Marco reconnects his Bluetooth headset.
- **Path:** 
  1. Marco clicks the play button on his headset.
  2. The application detects a 4-hour pause duration and calculates the dynamic auto-rewind offset (20 seconds back).
  3. Playback resumes smoothly from the adjusted position in file 18.
  4. The lock-screen widget and notification display the book title, current chapter title, and remaining time formatted as hours/minutes.
- **Climax:** Marco immediately recognizes the sentence where he left off without touching his phone screen.
- **Resolution:** Playback continues continuously across file boundaries; Marco arrives home and pauses.
- **Edge case:** If the calculated auto-rewind falls before the start of file 18, the player automatically positions into the tail of file 17 without audio stutter.

#### UJ-2. Elena Listens Before Sleep with Shake-to-Reset
- **Persona + context:** Elena listens to non-fiction before sleeping. The room is dark and she is drowsy.
- **Entry state:** In bed; phone resting on the nightstand; sleep timer configured for 15 minutes with motion tracking enabled.
- **Path:**
  1. Elena taps "Play" in the Bedside player screen. The sleep timer starts counting down from 15:00.
  2. At 14:00 elapsed (1 minute remaining), the audio begins a gradual volume fade-out, accompanied by a subtle soft notification tone.
  3. Elena is still awake and wants to keep listening; she reaches out and gives the phone a light shake.
  4. The accelerometer registers the motion exceeding the calibrated shake threshold.
  5. The player emits a gentle acknowledgement chime, restores playback volume to 100%, and resets the sleep countdown to 15:00.
- **Climax:** Elena extends her session without opening her eyes, unlocking the phone, or exposing her eyes to blue light.
- **Resolution:** 20 minutes later Elena falls asleep; the fade-out completes, playback halts, the audio engine unloads, and phone enters deep sleep.

#### UJ-3. Kenji Organizes Complex Character Casts and Notes
- **Persona + context:** Kenji is listening to a novel featuring over 40 Russian characters with complex patronymics.
- **Entry state:** Kenji hears a character mentioned whose relationship he forgot.
- **Path:**
  1. Kenji taps the Character button on the main player screen.
  2. A quick-search overlay displays alphabetical character cards loaded from `characters.sabp.xml`.
  3. Kenji searches "Dmitri", reads the note ("Eldest brother, former military officer"), and closes the modal.
  4. Kenji long-presses the Bookmark button to capture the current sentence as a reference point, entering a short note: *"Dispute over the inheritance"*.
- **Climax:** Kenji clarifies the plot point in under 10 seconds without losing his playback timestamp.
- **Resolution:** Kenji resumes playback; the bookmark is saved locally in `bookmarks.sabp.xml`.

#### UJ-4. Sara Reclaims Storage via Background Opus Conversion
- **Persona + context:** Sara has 64 GB of storage on an older phone and 40 GB of audiobook files in MP3 format.
- **Entry state:** Phone connected to home Wi-Fi and charger; Sara opens Settings > Convert MP3 to Opus.
- **Path:**
  1. Sara selects her audiobook root folder and enables "Convert MP3 to Opus".
  2. She sets the minimum battery safety threshold to 80% and keeps "Embed Cover Art" enabled.
  3. The conversion service runs in the background, transcoding files into standard 24-32kbps Opus speech containers.
  4. The system calculates accumulated space savings and updates the live counter: *"Saved: 18.4 GB"*.
  5. Original `.mp3` files are safely swapped for `.opus` files while preserving the exact playback position (`position.sabp.dat`).
- **Climax:** Sara frees up over half her device storage without breaking progress or losing metadata.
- **Resolution:** Sara opens the Library; all converted books continue playing seamlessly.

#### UJ-5. David Drives with Android Auto Integration
- **Persona + context:** David steps into his car for a 45-minute drive; phone connects to the dashboard head unit via Android Auto.
- **Entry state:** Android Auto launches; FolderPlay appears in the Media category.
- **Path:**
  1. The car display lists books grouped by "Started", "Queue", and "New".
  2. David taps the current book; playback begins via `MyMediaBrowserService`.
  3. The dashboard UI shows custom actions: -60s rewind, -10s rewind, +30s skip, Bookmark, and Play/Pause.
  4. The sleep timer is automatically disabled while connected to Android Auto to prevent sudden cutoffs while driving.
- **Climax:** David operates his audiobook safely using steering wheel controls and large automotive touch targets.
- **Resolution:** Upon disconnecting Bluetooth at arrival, playback pauses immediately and saves progress.

## 3. Glossary

- **Root Folder:** A user-selected parent directory on internal or external storage containing one or more audiobook folders.
- **Book (Entity):** A single directory under a root folder (or flattened folder) containing audio files, associated metadata files (`position.sabp.dat`, `bookmarks.sabp.xml`, `characters.sabp.xml`, `info.txt`), and artwork.
- **Book State:** The lifecycle state of a book:
  - `New`: Discovered in root folder but never played.
  - `Started`: Actively in progress with playback position > 0.
  - `Finished`: Playback completed to the final second of the last file, or marked finished manually.
- **Book Queue:** An ordered sequence of books determining which book starts automatically once the current book finishes.
- **Auto-Rewind:** An algorithmic adjustment that steps the playback timestamp backward upon resume, calculated as a non-linear function of elapsed pause duration.
- **RNNoise:** Recurrent Neural Network based real-time speech noise suppression library integrated via native C/JNI (`librnnoise.so`).
- **BASS Engine:** Low-level native audio library (`libbass.so` and plugins) providing tempo-preserving pitch scaling, volume boost, and format decoding.
- **Shake-to-Reset:** Using the device accelerometer sensor during sleep timer countdown or fadeout to reset the timer to its initial duration without touch input.
- **Fadeout:** A linear or exponential attenuation of audio output level over the final 60 seconds of a sleep timer cycle.
- **Global Chapter:** A unified chapter marker derived either from container metadata (M4B chapters, MP3 ID3 CHAP atoms) or external sidecar files (`libby.txt`, `.srt`).
- **Position Dat (`position.sabp.dat`):** Serialized binary state container preserving current file, position offset, speed, volume boost, and equalizer parameters per book.

## 4. Features & Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Dual Audio Engine & Sound Shaping

**Description:** The application utilizes a dual-engine architecture: a primary high-performance engine powered by Un4seen BASS (`libbass.so`) with specialized add-on plugins (`libbass_aac.so`, `libbassopus.so`, `libbassflac.so`, etc.) and a native Android `MediaPlayer` fallback with `AudioTrack` effects. Sound shaping includes pitch-invariant speed adjustment, independent pitch shift, hardware loudness enhancement, a multi-band equalizer, and neural noise suppression. Realizes UJ-1.

#### FR-1: Continuous Variable Playback Speed
Users can adjust audio speed from 0.5x to 3.0x in 0.05x increments without altering vocal pitch.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Modifying speed updates the BASS tempo channel without introducing harmonic distortion or chipmunk effect.
- Preferred speed is persisted both globally in default settings and per-book in `position.sabp.dat`.

#### FR-2: Independent Pitch Control
Users can shift voice pitch up or down (semitone adjustment) independently of playback rate.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Shifting pitch modifies fundamental vocal frequency while keeping duration and tempo constant.
- Setting is toggled and saved within `EqualizerLevels`.

#### FR-3: RNNoise Real-Time Noise Reduction
Users can toggle background hiss/hum suppression utilizing the embedded RNNoise recurrent neural network model.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Incoming audio buffer chunks (1920-byte frames) pass through `librnnoise.so`.
- Background microphone hum, tape hiss, and room resonance are attenuated while vocal formants remain intelligible.
- Full version gate check applies (`lv0.noise_reduction`).

#### FR-4: Volume Boost & Loudness Enhancer
Users can boost volume up to +20 dB beyond system maximum for quiet recordings.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Employs `LoudnessEnhancer` (or BASS pre-amplification gain) without hard clipping.
- Long-pressing the volume boost button steps gain down; short tapping steps gain up.

#### FR-5: Parametric Equalizer & Balance
Users can adjust multi-band EQ presets and channel balance (Left/Right) or downmix to Mono.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Equalizer settings persist per book and restore on playback launch.
- Mono downmix combines stereo tracks into single identical channels for single-earbud listening.

### 4.2 Auto-Rewind & Resumption Logic

**Description:** To prevent loss of narrative context after pauses, the app applies dynamic auto-rewind upon resuming playback. The rewind distance scales non-linearly with the duration of the pause. Realizes UJ-1.

#### FR-6: Pause-Proportional Auto-Rewind
The player must calculate the rewind offset based on elapsed wall-clock pause duration:
- Pauses < 3 seconds: 0 seconds rewind.
- Pauses between 3 seconds and 60 seconds: 2 to 5 seconds rewind.
- Pauses between 1 minute and 30 minutes: 10 to 15 seconds rewind.
- Pauses > 30 minutes: up to 30 seconds rewind (configurable ceiling).
**Consequences (testable):**
- System timestamp is recorded on pause (`System.currentTimeMillis()`).
- On resume, target position is set to `max(0, currentPosition - calculatedRewind)`.
- If rewind crosses the beginning of the current file, playback seamlessly rolls back into the preceding audio file at the corresponding offset.

#### FR-7: Short and Long Jump Navigation
Users can perform rapid jumps using dedicated forward and backward buttons with distinct small and big intervals.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Small jump default: 10 seconds rewind, 30 seconds forward (customizable in settings).
- Big jump default: 1 minute rewind, 5 minutes forward.
- Audio resumes immediately without pipeline stutter or UI freeze.

### 4.3 Sleep Timer & Motion-Aware Shake-to-Reset

**Description:** An advanced bedtime sleep management system allowing users to set timed sessions or play until end-of-file, complete with audio fade-out, audible warning cues, and hands-free accelerometer motion reset. Realizes UJ-2.

#### FR-8: Sleep Timer Duration Modes
Users can activate a sleep timer configured for:
- Standard durations: 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 minutes.
- End of File: Plays until the end of the currently playing audio file, then stops.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Time remaining is displayed on the main player interface and lock-screen notification.
- When timer reaches 00:00, playback transitions to pause and audio focus is abandoned.

#### FR-9: Gentle Audio Fadeout & Warning Chimes
During the final 60 seconds of the countdown, audio output volume attenuates linearly from 100% to 0%.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Optional subtle chime sounds at start of fadeout (`fadeoutNotificationVolume`).
- Prevents abrupt silence from startling the sleeping user.

#### FR-10: Accelerometer Shake-to-Reset
Users can reset the timer without touching the screen by moving or shaking the phone.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Sensitivity threshold is configurable (shake force levels: 1, 3, 6, 12, 18).
- Operational modes: `Always` (monitors throughout session), `During fadeout` (only during final minute), or `Never`.
- Successful shake resets timer to original duration and plays an optional confirmation chime (`resetNotificationVolume`).

#### FR-11: Scheduled Bedtime Sleep
Users can define an automatic sleep activation window (e.g., 23:00 to 07:00) where any playback started within this interval automatically activates the sleep timer.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Starts sleep timer automatically without manual user action if current time falls within schedule.
- Android Auto override: sleep timer automatically disables when connected to automotive head units (`turnOffSleepTimerInAndroidAuto`).

### 4.4 Library Organization, Classification & File System Model

**Description:** The application operates directly against user-designated root folders on the device or SD card. It treats folders as books, classifies progress automatically, supports multi-part tracks, and stores all metadata in human-readable or standard local files directly inside the book directory. Realizes UJ-4.

#### FR-12: Root Folder Management & Storage Access Framework (SAF)
Users can add multiple storage directories (internal storage, micro-SD cards, USB OTG drives) as library roots.
**Consequences (testable):**
- App scans root folders recursively for audio formats (`.mp3`, `.m4b`, `.m4a`, `.opus`, `.ogg`, `.flac`, `.aac`, `.wma`, `.wav`, etc.).
- Folders containing supported audio files are parsed as independent Books.
- Non-audio media is ignored except recognized sidecars (`info.txt`, `libby.txt`, `cover.jpg`, `cover.png`, `.srt`).

#### FR-13: Book Lifecycle Classification
Books are categorized into three distinct tabs/filters:
1. `New`: Never played; progress = 0.
2. `Started`: Playback has begun; progress > 0 and not marked finished.
3. `Finished`: Played to completion or manually flagged finished.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Playing a `New` book immediately transitions its state to `Started`.
- Completing the final track prompts the user: *"The book is finished. Choose another?"* and marks the book `Finished`.
- Users can manually change status via context menu.

#### FR-14: Multi-File Book Assembly & Numerical Sorting
Books split across multiple files (e.g., `Part01.mp3`, `Part02.mp3` or `Chapter_01.opus`) are assembled into a single continuous timeline.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Natural alphanumeric filename sorting (`v7` natural sort comparator) ensures `File 2` plays before `File 10`.
- Total book duration is the computed sum of all individual file durations.
- Transitioning across file boundaries is gapless and automatic.

#### FR-15: Book Queue Management
Users can construct a playback queue specifying which book plays next when the active book completes.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Long-pressing a book in Library allows adding to queue.
- When active book finishes, player automatically loads index 0 of queue and begins playback if configured.

#### FR-16: Local Metadata Persistence (`.sabp` Sidecars)
All player progress, bookmarks, and character notes must be saved directly into the book's directory:
- `position.sabp.dat`: Current file, millisecond position, volume, speed, EQ.
- `bookmarks.sabp.xml`: Bookmark positions, timestamps, user titles, and descriptions.
- `characters.sabp.xml`: Character list names and biographical descriptions.
- `info.txt`: Plaintext book summary or synopsis.
**Consequences (testable):**
- Moving a book directory to another device running FolderPlay (or Smart AudioBook Player) fully preserves playback position, bookmarks, and notes without cloud sync.
- Writes to sidecar files utilize atomic temporary file replacement (`.tmp` -> target file rename) to prevent state corruption during sudden OS process termination or battery depletion.
- If a sidecar file is malformed, corrupt, or unreadable, the system logs a non-fatal warning, initializes a clean default state, and preserves the uncorrupted audio files without interrupting playback.

## 5. Non-Goals (Explicit)

- **Cloud Sync / Proprietary Cloud Accounts:** The app will *not* introduce user registration, server-side database accounts, or proprietary sync servers. All state stays on local storage or user-controlled backups.
- **In-App Audiobook Store / DRM Decryption:** The app will *not* sell audiobooks or include decryption modules for proprietary DRM formats.
- **Social Media & Community Feeds:** The app will *not* include reading feeds, comment sections, or social sharing networks.
- **Streaming Web Radio / Podcasts:** The app will *not* function as an RSS podcast aggregator or streaming radio player.

## 7. Success Metrics & Counter-Metrics

### 7.1 Primary Metrics
- **SM-1 (Position Resumption Reliability):** 99.99% of session resumes accurately hit the calculated auto-rewind timestamp without audio buffer loss or file corruption. Validates FR-6, FR-16.
- **SM-2 (Sleep Timer Touchless Recovery):** > 85% of extended sleep sessions are successfully triggered via accelerometer shake without screen illumination. Validates FR-10.
- **SM-3 (Cold-Start Library Indexing Performance):** Library scanning parses 500 multi-part books (10,000 files) in under 3.5 seconds on mid-tier Android devices. Validates FR-12, FR-14.

### 7.2 Counter-Metrics (Do Not Optimize)
- **SM-C1 (Battery Overhead vs Audio Quality):** Do not over-process audio filters or motion polling at the expense of background battery drain. Player background idle power draw must stay < 1.5% per hour. Counterbalances FR-3, FR-10.
- **SM-C2 (Zero Storage Lock-in):** Do not replace standard open `.txt`, `.xml`, or `.dat` files with closed proprietary binary databases that prevent users from manually moving book folders between devices. Counterbalances FR-16.

## 8. Open Questions

1. **Flutter Audio Engine Strategy:** Should the Flutter implementation use `audio_service` + `just_audio` with native DSP audio sinks, or bind directly to Un4seen BASS / RNNoise via Dart FFI (`dart:ffi`) for byte-level sample processing?
2. **Modern Scoped Storage in Flutter:** Android 13+ (API 33+) restrictions require SAF tree persistence. On Flutter, should this be bridged via `shared_storage` / platform channels or native Android DocumentFile interop to preserve sidecar write performance?
3. **Opus Transcoder Viability:** Is native Opus conversion still critical given current device storage capacities (128GB - 512GB standard), or should it be modernized as an optional external plugin?
